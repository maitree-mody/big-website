There are two ways to be part of Barnard Investment Group: general body membership, open to the Columbia community, and the executive board.

General Body

  1. Subscribe to the weekly newsletter for the event calendar.
  2. Attend three events in a semester, including speaker events, general body meetings, workshops, or site visits.
  3. Become a member, with priority access to site visits and opportunities.

Questions

FAQ

Do I need prior finance experience?

No. We look for students who are genuinely interested in finance. Following the markets is helpful, but many members begin with no background, and our workshops are designed for them.

Do I have to apply to join the general body?

No. There is no application. Attend three BIG events in a semester and you become a general body member.

Who can attend BIG events?

All students in the Columbia community, including Barnard, Columbia College, SEAS, and GS, are welcome at our events and in the general body. The executive board is made up of Barnard students.

How do I hear about upcoming events?

The weekly newsletter is the best way. All events are also posted on Instagram.
