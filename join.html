FAQ

Do I need finance experience?

No. We look for people who are genuinely interested in finance. Following the markets is a plus, but plenty of members start from zero, and that's what our workshops are for.

Do I have to apply to join the general body?

Nope, there's no application. Attend three BIG events in a semester and you're a general body member.

Who can come to BIG events?

Everyone in the Columbia community. Barnard, Columbia College, SEAS and GS students are all welcome at our events and in the general body. Our executive board is made up of Barnard students.

How do I hear about events?

The weekly newsletter is the best way, and we post everything on Instagram too.
