Barnard Investment Group

Barnard College's Premier Finance Organization

A supportive community for Barnard and Columbia women interested in finance, helping them build on their curiosity and deepen their understanding of financial markets.

About Us

Barnard Investment Group is Barnard College's first and only investment club. Founded in spring 2023 to close the gap in finance resources for Barnard students, BIG brings industry professionals to campus, organizes visits to leading financial institutions, and teaches the technical and personal-finance skills that a career in finance requires. Our events are open to the entire Columbia community.
