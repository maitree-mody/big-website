About BIG

Built by Barnard women, for Barnard women.

Our story

Barnard's first investment club.

Barnard Investment Group was founded in spring 2023 to close a gap. Barnard students recruiting for finance were doing it without a finance major, without a shared Handshake with Columbia, and often without anyone to ask. BIG set out to build that network.

Today we bring industry professionals to campus, take members inside the firms they're recruiting for, and teach the technicals and personal-finance skills every career in finance starts with. Barnard students lead the club, and our events are open to the whole Columbia community.
