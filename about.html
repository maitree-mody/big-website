About BIG

Founded in spring 2023, Barnard Investment Group is the first investment club based at Barnard College.

History

Our story

Barnard Investment Group was founded to address a gap in finance resources for Barnard students. Without a finance major, a shared recruiting platform with Columbia, or an established alumni network in the industry, Barnard students were often recruiting without support.

Today, BIG brings industry professionals to campus, organizes visits to leading financial institutions, and teaches the technical and personal-finance skills that a career in finance requires. The club is led by Barnard students, and its events are open to the entire Columbia community.

Our programs

I

Speaker Series

Fireside chats and panels with professionals from BlackRock, KKR, Blackstone, Morgan Stanley, and other leading firms.

II

Site Visits

Office visits to Goldman Sachs, Bank of America, and HSBC, with panels and Q&As alongside each firm's professionals.

Career Development

Technical training, resume reviews, and recruiting Q&As led by upperclassmen who have completed the process.

IV

Financial Literacy

Personal finance and investing workshops, mentorship, and partner programs such as Girls Who Invest.
